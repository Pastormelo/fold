Fold

shepherding care, kept honest

Three takes on the navigation structure, all mobile first. Each one answers the same Monday-morning question differently: who in my fold has gone quiet? Same visual language, same data model, different spine. Pick a spine and I will build it out into the full flow, then the web view for staff.

Fold first

Tabbed. Your people are the home screen.

Opens straight into your assigned fold, sorted by silence rather than alphabet. A coverage bar at the top is the accountability number an elder reports on at the monthly meeting. Tabs keep the feed, tasks and directory one thumb away.

One stream

No tabs. One page that answers, then gets out of the way.

A single scroll of what today asks of you, each item resolvable inline with one tap. Everything else lives behind one pill at the bottom: tap to capture, swipe up to jump anywhere. This is the version volunteers will actually keep using, and the fastest path from conversation to logged note.
