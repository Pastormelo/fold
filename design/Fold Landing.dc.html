The honest problem

Care lives in three places, and none of them is a system.

A spreadsheet nobody updates
Rows and columns are for numbers. People do not fit in them, so after two weeks the updates stop.
Forty minutes of staff meeting
If you miss the meeting, you miss the care. And whatever was said in June is already gone.
One leader's memory
The most common care system in the church, and the one that leaves when they do.
How it works

Three habits, and the rest takes care of itself.

01 · Open
See who has gone quiet
Your fold, sorted by silence instead of alphabet. The three people nobody has spoken to are at the top, every time you open it.
02 · Engage
Log it in ten seconds
One tap for "engaged." Dictate a note in the car. Tag it, choose who can see it, done before you reach the driveway.
03 · Account
Report without a spreadsheet
Your monthly elders report writes itself from what was actually logged, fold by fold, in your shepherds' own words.
Shepherding folds

Every person has a name attached to them.

Divide the church into folds and give each one a leader: an elder, a group leader, a care volunteer. Not a category, not a tag, but a person who is responsible, with co-shepherds beside them and a coverage number they can answer for.

One fold per person, so care is never assumed to be someone else's job.
Fold health at a glance, so an overloaded leader is visible before they burn out.
Move a household between folds and the whole care history goes with them.
Care journeys
Grief, hospital, marital challenges, personal struggles, new believer, restoration: steps with owners and dates, not a pile of notes.
Nudges that name a person
When a follow-up goes stale, the leader who owns it hears about it. Not a group text nobody reads.
Milestones you would hate to miss
Birthdays, anniversaries, and the anniversary of a loss, which matters more than the other two.
Confidentiality

A memory, not a file on someone.

Every note carries a visibility tier: all leaders, staff, or elders only. Confidential matters stay out of the open feed and out of reports read aloud in a room. A group leader sees their fold and nothing else.

All leaders
Visits, check-ins, milestones. The ordinary work of care.
Staff
Situations the office needs to handle well and quietly.
Elders only
Restoration, benevolence, counseling. Locked, logged, and never in the feed.
