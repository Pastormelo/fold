One mark, not two things

Two ways to close the gap. 3a makes the fold the letter O, so the mark and the word are one object and the stray sheep escapes over the top. 3b keeps them separate but tightens the lockup to cap height with a proper optical gap. 3a is applied to the app, the web view and the landing page.
